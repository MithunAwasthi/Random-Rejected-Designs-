# Random-Rejected-Designs-
This design was part of a concept pitch or client proposal that didn’t make the final cut. 
This folder contains design concepts that were pitched but not selected — whether due to client feedback, direction changes, or creative experimentation. While these designs didn’t make it to the final stage, they reflect valuable parts of the process:
• 	🎨 Alternative visual styles and bold ideas
• 	🧠 Early-stage thinking and concept development
• 	🔄 Iterative design choices and refinement
• 	📚 Lessons learned and inspiration for future work
Rejection isn’t failure — it’s part of growth. These designs helped shape stronger outcomes and showcase my range as a creative professional.
